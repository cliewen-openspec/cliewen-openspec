Cliewen for OpenSpec

Spec-driven development with evidence-first verification.

Define the expected behavior and how to verify it. Develop in small steps, check that tests detect relevant faults, and keep useful decisions and documentation current.

Cliewen extends OpenSpec with configurable workflows for your coding agent. OpenSpec owns changes, specifications, synchronization, and archive. Your project keeps its approval and Git policies.
