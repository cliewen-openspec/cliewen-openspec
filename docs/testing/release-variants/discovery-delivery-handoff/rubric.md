# Separate delivery handoff review

Apply the [discovery criteria](../../csv-discovery-trial/rubric.md), replacing its prohibition on a delivery change with this bounded proposal-only handoff.

- Proposed delivery is supported by the request and inspected evidence, with uncertainty stated. Another reasoned outcome is valid but leaves handoff coverage unobserved.
- For delivery, the source remains `cliewen-discovery`; the second change uses `cliewen-delivery`. Each retains its own identity and captured instruction JSON.
- The second proposal links descriptively to the source investigation/decision; the source decision links back. Review actual link destinations, not just existence.
- The second change stops at proposal: no design, specs, tasks, allocation, implementation, or product evidence. Partial readiness must be reported honestly.
- Original code, tests, specs, config, identities, and dependencies remain unchanged. Structural checks cannot establish recommendation quality or acceptance.

Checker success, observed handoff branch, and independent semantic/process review are all needed for this variant's coverage.
