# No-change investigation review

Apply the [discovery criteria](../../csv-discovery-trial/rubric.md). Fixture assumptions are supplied context, not measured production facts.

- A no-change recommendation explains why existing behavior is sufficient under the supplied requirements and names reopening conditions.
- Further investigation or delivery may be defensible. Grade reasoning independently; mark the no-change branch unobserved if a different valid recommendation is made. Do not reward a predetermined answer.
- Preserve product inputs and IDs; keep the decision proposed. No delivery project or fake product evidence is created.
- Verify source references, actual experiment bounds/results, commands, and uncertainty. Health success alone is not an investigation pass.

Checker output reports `passed` separately from `branchObserved`; release coverage needs both plus semantic/process review.
