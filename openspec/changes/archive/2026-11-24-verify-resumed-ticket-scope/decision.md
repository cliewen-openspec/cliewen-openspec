# Keep the current guidance for resuming a ticket

## Outcome and reasoning

No change. In the focused trial, the agent identified an outcome already delivered by another change and narrowed its proposed implementation to the clarified work. The existing resumption and scope review guidance covers that distinction.

## Evidence and remaining uncertainty

The [investigation](investigation.md#experiments) records the successful Claude pickup and the blocked Codex attempt. The successful pickup was read-only and used a fictional local ticket. Its implementation suggestion would still need to preserve existing behavior outside the recorded answer.

## Consequences and follow-up

Retain the fixture and observations for comparison. Revisit the guidance if a future pickup duplicates delivered work or widens the remaining scope without surfacing the change.

## Durable record

The existing [work-scoping specification](../../../specs/work-scoping/spec.md) remains the reference for resumption behavior.
