# Link the solo-to-team walkthrough to its requirements

## Outcome and reasoning

Delivery handoff for a small documentation change. Add direct links to the permanent requirements for switching profiles and reserving IDs across clones. The walkthrough already explains when each applies.

## Evidence and remaining uncertainty

The [navigation trial](investigation.md#experiments) resolved both links from the existing walkthrough. It covered one archived change. It did not establish that other journeys need the same links or that the workflow needs a general index.

## Consequences and follow-up

Keep the links beside the relevant profile and coordination guidance. Verify that they work in the repository and the published guide build. Keep the behavioral requirements in their permanent specs.

## Durable record

The walkthrough is the existing home for this journey. Its links can carry the result.
