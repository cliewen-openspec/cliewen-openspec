# change-closure Specification

## Purpose
Preserve useful project knowledge and report actual closure outcomes around OpenSpec archive using the project's existing settings and policies.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Proportionate pre-archive review
Closure guidance SHALL review affected specs, durable documents, decisions, tests, reusable lessons, and unresolved work according to existing feature and policy settings. It SHALL preserve project authorities and paths and require new records only when useful.

#### Scenario: Delivery changes accepted behavior
- **WHEN** a delivery is ready for archive
- **THEN** guidance reconciles its actual scope with affected documentation and decisions and applies existing test lifecycle and evidence policies
- **AND** shared active assertions and reserved identities remain protected during retirement

#### Scenario: Disabled facilities
- **WHEN** planning, documentation, decisions, learnings, or lifecycle review is disabled under its existing settings
- **THEN** the corresponding optional work is skipped and authored files remain intact
- **AND** enabled evidence checks and existing outcome and approval boundaries still apply

#### Scenario: No useful new document
- **WHEN** closure produces no durable decision or reusable lesson
- **THEN** guidance does not require a new record, note, empty index, or separate closure artifact

### Requirement: Supported archive-session access
The package SHALL provide an installed closure reference, shared apply guidance, and an opt-in example using native OpenSpec archive operation guidance. The example SHALL preserve existing project guidance and explain its advisory agent-level scope and version limits.

#### Scenario: Separate archive session
- **WHEN** a project follows the documented opt-in setup on supported OpenSpec
- **THEN** native archive instructions expose an entry pointing to the installed reference while retaining existing entries
- **AND** guidance applies to Cliewen workflow changes and respects native paths, choices, and archive behavior

#### Scenario: Direct CLI or unavailable operation inputs
- **WHEN** archive is performed directly through the CLI or the operation-guidance lookup is unavailable
- **THEN** documentation explains that closure work must be requested separately and is not executed automatically by the archive command

### Requirement: Outcome-appropriate closure
Guidance SHALL distinguish delivered behavior from investigation findings and no-delivery outcomes. Discovery and combined no-delivery closure SHALL preserve useful findings and unresolved uncertainty using their existing outcome contracts.

#### Scenario: Investigation ends without delivery
- **WHEN** discovery or a combined no-delivery outcome is archived
- **THEN** guidance retains actual findings, cleanup outcomes, decision status, and relevant follow-up without requesting product execution evidence for unimplemented behavior

### Requirement: Reassess future work after actual completion
With planning enabled, guidance SHALL reassess affected future work after successful archive against actual outcomes, dependencies, and project completion criteria. It SHALL use the selected authority and existing ticket-access and authorization rules.

#### Scenario: Next item no longer holds
- **WHEN** the delivered result invalidates a later plan item's premise
- **THEN** guidance records or proposes the appropriate revision at the authority and seeks direction for material changes in agreed scope
- **AND** it prepares the next detailed proposal only when that work is selected

#### Scenario: Ticket cannot be updated
- **WHEN** the selected ticket is read-only, inaccessible, or not authorized for update
- **THEN** guidance reports the actual archive outcome and the concrete pending update separately
- **AND** archive alone is not reported as ticket completion or Git integration

### Requirement: Report actual state and evidence freshness
Guidance SHALL report archive location, actual spec synchronization, remaining work, and relevant verification limits in a brief handoff. It SHALL preserve revision freshness rules and distinguish successful archive from partial or failed operations.

#### Scenario: Archive succeeds and tracked inputs change
- **WHEN** archive or subsequent documentation edits change tested inputs
- **THEN** guidance finishes intended tracked edits before committing and recollecting required permanent-spec evidence
- **AND** earlier evidence is identified as historical until refreshed

#### Scenario: Archive or synchronization fails
- **WHEN** the native operation fails or completes only part of the intended work
- **THEN** guidance inspects the reported result and current paths, reports what actually changed and what remains, and does not claim full closure or blindly repeat the operation
